# ADR-0002: Repository Standards

Status: Accepted

## Decision

MarketPilot AI will keep a stable repository structure after Sprint 1.

## Rules

- Keep documentation under `docs/`.
- Keep strategy documents under `strategy/`.
- Keep AI design documents under `ai/`.
- Keep project templates under `.github/`.

## Consequence

After Sprint 1, repository structure changes should be rare and intentional.
