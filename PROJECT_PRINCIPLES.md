# PROJECT PRINCIPLES

Version: 0.0.1

## Purpose
MarketPilot AI exists to help investors make disciplined, data-driven and explainable investment decisions.

## Core Principles
1. Protect capital first.
2. Every recommendation must be explainable.
3. No emotional trading.
4. Data before opinion.
5. Risk management is mandatory.
6. Learn from every trade.
7. Transparency over black-box decisions.
8. Modular architecture.
9. Validate before automating.
10. Simplicity wins.

## Motto
Decisions backed by data.
Executed with discipline.
